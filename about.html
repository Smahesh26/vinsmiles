The Founding Story

"I didn't start VinSmiles to build a practice. I started it to change an experience."

Dr. Neha Sachdeva has been practicing dentistry since 2009, and established VinSmiles in 2019 with a belief that was, frankly, radical at the time: that patients should want to come to the dentist. Not reluctantly, not anxiously — but with confidence, curiosity, and the expectation of being genuinely cared for.

The dental sector had a problem. Not with clinical quality — India had extraordinary dentists — but with how care was delivered. Cold environments. Rushed consultations. Patients who left knowing what had been done to them, but not why. Dental anxiety, especially among women and children, was near-universal. VinSmiles was built to actively alleviate dental anxiety at every step.

"
I founded VinSmiles to do two things — change how people feel about dentists, and show what women in dentistry can achieve.
Dr. Neha Sachdeva · Founder & Lead Prosthodontist

Twenty years on, VinSmiles is South Delhi's most trusted specialist dental practice — a clinic where seven in-house specialists work under one roof, where technology is deployed not to impress but to serve precision, and where every patient walks out knowing exactly what their smile journey looks like.

The clinic has treated patients from more than fifteen countries, pioneered dental tourism protocols for international visitors, and built a team that matches the clinical depth of practices charging three times the price. But the thing Dr. Neha remains most proud of? The fact that patients don't dread coming back.

The VinSmiles Philosophy

Three beliefs that shape every decision we make — from how we hire specialists to how we design every appointment.

01
Comfort First
Dental anxiety is real — and preventable. Every aspect of VinSmiles is engineered to remove it. From the clinic's lighting and interiors to the way consultations are conducted, comfort is non-negotiable before clinical excellence is even discussed.
02
Specialist Excellence
No generalists. Every treatment at VinSmiles is performed by a specialist — someone who has trained specifically in that discipline and does it every day. Your implant is placed by an implantologist. Your aligners are monitored by an orthodontist.
03
Technology Forward
Every procedure we offer is backed by the diagnostic and treatment technology it deserves. CBCT imaging. Digital Smile Design. Intraoral scanning. Laser precision. Not as features — as the standard of care every patient should expect.
