Our Treatments

Comprehensive Care,
Expert Hands

Every treatment at VinSmiles is performed by a dedicated specialist. Not a generalist. Your case gets the right hands from day one.

01 Dental Implants & Full Restoration +

Titanium implants, All-on-4, and full-arch solutions — placed by our specialist oral implantologist with CBCT-guided surgery and digital precision planning. Success rate 97%+.

Explore Implants
02 Digital Smile Design +

See your final smile before any treatment begins. Our DSD workflow creates a digital blueprint of your ideal result — approved by you before we start.

Explore DSD
03 Invisalign & Clear Aligners +

Certified Invisalign provider with iTero digital scanning. Straighten invisibly, comfortably, and on your schedule — with clinically tracked monthly progress.

Explore Aligners
04 Porcelain Veneers +

Ultra-thin, stain-resistant porcelain veneers custom-crafted for your facial structure. Options include Emax and Emax CAD restorations, with Lumineers considered where clinically relevant. Designed for natural aesthetics and long-term durability.

Explore Veneers
05 Full Mouth Rehabilitation +

Multi-specialist, comprehensive restoration for patients with extensive dental needs using facebow transfer and virtual articulator planning for precise bite rehabilitation. One coordinated team. One treatment plan. Total transformation.

Explore FMR
06 Laser Dentistry +

Triple wavelength diode laser for gum contouring, depigmentation, crown lengthening, root canal adjunct procedures, teeth whitening, and soft-tissue management — minimal discomfort, faster healing, exceptional results.

Explore Laser
07 Gum Treatment & Periodontics +

Our specialist periodontist with 15+ years handles everything from routine maintenance to advanced flap surgery — the foundation every great smile is built on.

Explore Periodontics
