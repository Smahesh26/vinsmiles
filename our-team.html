Dr. Neha Sachdeva

MDS (Prosthodontics) -- Oral Implantology -- Invisalign Certified -- Practicing Since 2009

Dr. Neha Sachdeva has been practicing dentistry since 2009 and established VIN SMILES in 2019 with a single conviction: that world-class dental care should be accessible, honest, and deeply human. A specialist prosthodontist with postgraduate training in oral implantology, she has built the clinic around her own standard -- one that refuses to separate clinical precision from personal warmth. Her work spans complex full mouth rehabilitations, smile makeover planning, implant-supported restorations, and alleviating the dental anxiety for first-time and long-term patients.
