Comprehensive Care

Complete Treatment Catalog

Dental Implants

Dental implant surgery replaces tooth roots with metal posts and damaged or missing teeth with artificial teeth that look and function much like real ones.

  • Conventional & Immediate Implants
  • Missing Tooth Replacement
  • All on Six / All on Eight
  • Sinus Lift & Bone Grafting

Prosthodontics

Rehabilitation and maintenance of oral function, comfort, and appearance through advanced restorative techniques.

  • Fixed Partial Dentures
  • Porcelain & Metal-Free Crowns
  • Complete & BPS Dentures
  • Implant Supported Over Dentures

Conservative Dentistry

Focuses on preserving as much of the natural tooth structure as possible while restoring damaged or decayed teeth.

  • Tooth Colored Restorations
  • Fluoride Application
  • Pit and Fissure Sealants

Endodontics

Expert diagnosis and treatment of dental pulp and the tissues surrounding the roots of the teeth.

  • Single Visit Root Canal Treatment
  • Post and Core Build Ups
  • Dental Crowns

Periodontics

Prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of diseases that affect the gums and supporting structures of the teeth.

  • Flap Surgery & Root Planning
  • Gingivectomy & Crown Lengthening
  • De-Pigmentation & Ultrasonic Cleaning
  • Advanced Laser Gum Surgery

Oral Surgery

Specialized diagnosis and surgical treatment of diseases, injuries, and deformities of the oral and maxillofacial region.

  • Painless Wisdom Tooth Removal
  • Surgical Extraction
  • Jaw Cysts Treatment
  • Minor Surgical Procedures

Orthodontics

A specialized branch focusing on the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of dental and facial irregularities.

  • Fixed Braces & Pre Treatment Trainers
  • Invisible Aligners (Invisalign)
  • Habit Breaking Appliances

Smile Designing

Advanced cosmetic dentistry offering customized Digital Smile Design for perfect aesthetic outcomes.

  • Digital Smile Design
  • Dental Laminates & Veneers
  • Lumineers & Emax Crowns
  • Tooth Jewelry & Whitening

Full Mouth Rehab

A comprehensive, multidisciplinary treatment plan that aims to fully restore the function, health, and appearance of the entire mouth.

  • Thorough Oral Examination
  • Complex, Multidisciplinary Care
  • Full Restoration of Function
  • Complete Aesthetic Reconstruction

Pedodontics

Gentle dental care and treatment of children, including preventive and therapeutic oral healthcare for infants and teenagers.

  • Pulpectomy & Pulpotomy
  • SS & Strip Crowns
  • Space Maintainers & Fluoride
  • Habit Breaking & Myofunctional Appliances
